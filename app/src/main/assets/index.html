          On-device AI

          Checking…

          Photos are analyzed by Gemini Nano on this phone. Nothing is sent to a server and there is no per-photo cost.

          Protein, calories, carbs and fat for common foods come from a built-in nutrition table (USDA and IFCT style values). Foods not in the table use the model's own estimate and are marked "AI estimate". Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts.

          Backup

          Your log lives only on this phone. Save a backup to Drive or email it to yourself now and then.

          Estimates are approximate. Hidden oil, sauces and portion size can move them a lot, so use the package label when you have one.

          Photos never leave your phone. Your log keeps a small thumbnail, not the full photo.

          Protein targets in the calculator follow common sports-nutrition ranges. If you have kidney disease, ask your doctor before raising protein.
